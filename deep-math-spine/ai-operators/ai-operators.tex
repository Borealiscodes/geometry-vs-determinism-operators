\subsection{Context Transport Operator}
\begin{definition}
A \emph{context transport operator} $\mathcal{T}$ acts on a reasoning
state $(p,q)$ consisting of geometric position $p \in \M$ and discrete
state $q \in V(G)$, producing
\[
\mathcal{T}(p,q) = (\Psi(p,q),\, q)
\]
where $\Psi$ is a geometric update rule influenced by hybrid or spectral
structure.
\end{definition}
